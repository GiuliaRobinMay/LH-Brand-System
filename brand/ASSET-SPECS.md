# Community asset specs

Every branded surface in the Mighty Networks community, with its required size.
Source: the Mighty Networks Branding Guide deck.

**Every asset uses `brand/tokens.css` for colour and the type scale in `TYPOGRAPHY.md`.**
Matthew appears per `brand/character/COSTUMES.md` — the right costume for the job.

---

## Network-level

| Asset | Minimum size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Network Logo | 500 × 500 | Square. Corners are rounded on crop — keep padding at the edges. |
| Primary Image / Video | 1920 × 1080 | Responsive; edges crop on other screen sizes. Keep text well inside. |
| Global Header Brand | 1155 × 245 | Replaces the logo in the web nav bar and mobile title bar. Sits on the Header Color. |

Colours: Basic allows one primary; **Advanced allows up to three** — use Advanced.

## Space-level

| Asset | Minimum size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Space Logo | 500 × 500 | Rounded-square crop. Optional separate dark-mode version. Reads small in the sidebar — keep it simple. |
| Space Branding Image | 1155 × 245 | Replaces the Space logo, name and tagline in the title bar. |
| Space Banner — Thin | 2880 × 150 | Full width, fixed height. Best for colour and pattern, not detail. |
| Space Banner — Tall | 1560 × 334 | Fixed aspect ratio. Best when the image has recognisable content. |

## Rules that apply across every asset

**Text safe areas are not optional.** Several assets crop differently by device. Where the
deck names a safe area, keep all type inside it.

**The accessibility floor still applies** — it does not relax because something is a
banner. Type sized for a thumbnail must still be legible on a phone; contrast pairs come
from `tokens.css` and the forbidden pairings are forbidden here too.

**Small assets carry one idea.** Space logos, badges and 80 × 80 feature icons are seen at
a fraction of their exported size. One shape, one colour, no text.

**The banner pair is a choice of job, not taste.** Thin (2880 × 150) is for colour and
pattern; Tall (1560 × 334) is for imagery with content. Putting a detailed illustration in
a thin banner wastes it.
