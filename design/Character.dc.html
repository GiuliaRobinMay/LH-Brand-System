AI photoreal — never
It is precisely what the impersonators produce. Hard rule.
Wardrobe rules
The motif is always question marks — never blocks or abstract shapes.
The shirt ground is always black. Never a plain white shirt.
The shirt is never plainer than the suit. He doesn't do quiet underneath.
Nothing corporate below the neck — no ties, no plain black belt.
Generating him — the three that matter
1. Never generate from text alone — always attach a reference still.
2. Never ban his features. Spiky hair and the open laugh are the character.
3. Never write his real name in a prompt.
Wild hair and a big open laugh are not the problem. Unsmiling eyes are.
