Before you start
The ACP program ended in 2024 — ignore any site still advertising its $30 internet credit. A real program never charges a fee just to apply.

Lower your phone or internet bill
Lifeline is the main federal program. It gives a monthly discount on phone or internet through participating providers.
Lifeline — federal phone & internet discount
Up to $9.25/month off, or $34.25 on Tribal lands.
www.lifelinesupport.org
2
Free or low-cost phones
Some Lifeline providers include a free phone with the monthly discount.
Lifeline provider search
Find companies in your state — many include a free phone.
www.lifelinesupport.org/companies-near-me

A tip that helps
One Lifeline discount per household, and you choose phone or internet. Pair it with your provider's own low-income plan to save the most.
