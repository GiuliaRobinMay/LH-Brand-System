How objects behave
Flat fill, one brand colour each, bold black outline of even weight. Scattered with generous air, never crowded, never overlapping the type. Rotated a few degrees so they feel dropped, not placed. Never more than six on a surface. They decorate the space around the message — they never compete with it.
